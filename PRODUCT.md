# SunCredit: product context

Inferred from the written brief (no interview possible; the brief answers the main questions).

## What it is
A carbon-credit registry for small Nigerian solar sites that replace diesel generators. Owners send energy and fuel records, verifiers check them, buyers purchase whole-tonne credits, and the money is split back to the owners, verifiers and the platform.

## Users and scenes
- **Owner**: a shop, pharmacy or home owner in Lagos, Kano, Ibadan, Abuja. Mostly on a mid-range Android phone, in bright daylight, on patchy data. Not a carbon expert. Needs to know "what did I earn, what is blocking me".
- **Verifier**: at a laptop or tablet, reviewing a queue. Needs evidence in one place, fast, with a clear audit trail.
- **Buyer**: a company sustainability lead. Needs stock, a clear price, a certificate they can show.
- **Admin / demo operator**: resets data, forces test outcomes, reads the audit log.
- **Public**: anyone looking up a certificate serial. No login, no personal data.

## Mode
Operate (app UI) for every signed-in screen. The public certificate page is Read.

## Tone
Plain, exact, unhurried. Money and tonnes are never rounded casually. Errors name the problem and the way out.

## Design principles
1. Every number can be traced: the claim page shows each factor used.
2. State is always visible: where a month is in the pipeline, who holds it, what is next.
3. Phone first for owners and buyers; dense and keyboard-friendly for verifiers.
4. Motion explains state change (a sun moving along the pipeline, a stamp on approval) and never decorates.
5. Never label real-looking data as anything other than what it is in the product UI; configuration lives on the admin page.

## Anti-references
Generic crypto/eco-green landing gradients, stock leaf icons, dashboards of identical metric cards.
